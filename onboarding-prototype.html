Rubi insights Three things worth knowing before you start
Learning tip · spacing

Short and often beats one long sitting

Three 30-minute sessions across a week stick better than one 90-minute cram, even if the total time is the same. Compass is built around that rhythm.

Learning tip · testing yourself

Testing yourself works better than re-reading

Pulling an answer out of your head — even getting it wrong — makes it stick better than reading the material a second time. That's why flashcards and checks show up as often as reading.

Learning tip · knowing what you know

The topics you feel most sure about often hide the biggest gaps

When Rubi spots a gap between how you rate yourself and how you actually score, that's the best place to spend time — not the topics that already feel hard.
